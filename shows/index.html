Good to know

Before you enter

Entries

Each show's Enter link takes you to its entry page. New to the series? Get in touch and we'll walk you through signing up.

Fees

Class fees are posted with each show. Pay per class — no membership and no season commitment required.

Location

Every show runs at Ashland Farm in Walnut Grove, Georgia — serving the Athens & Covington area.

Big Cheese Eventing

A local schooling show series for the Athens & Covington area of Georgia. Run by Caroline Templeton.
